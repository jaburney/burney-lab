We are always glad to hear from strong postdoctoral candidates, and we're happy to work with people on fellowship applications well ahead of deadlines. All specific advertised openings are linked below.

Beyond already funded / advertised positions, we regularly support candidates applying for their own external fellowship funding or applying through a number of internal programs. Please check out the following for potential fit:

Postdocs in the group both lead and contribute to group research, publish as lead authors, and mentor students. Some of our group projects involve fieldwork and international collaboration; others are entirely computational. We welcome postdoctoral candidates using these or other complementary approaches. Please reach out with the following information if you are interested in joining us:

1. A short description of the research you want to do over the next 2-3 years.
2. How that connects to the group's work, and what you'd bring that's new.
3. Your CV, and one or two representative papers (published or in preparation).
4. Your timeline: when you expect to defend or finish your current position, and any fellowship deadlines you're working toward.
5. Names of two or three references.

Note: we do not respond to generic mass blasts but are always delighted to respond to genuine individual inquiries.
