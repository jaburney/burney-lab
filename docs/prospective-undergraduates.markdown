I am happy to serve as a mentor for current Stanford undergraduate students through Stanford's paid undergraduate research programs:

{: .program-list}
- <span class="program-name">[King Center Academic Year Part-Time Undergraduate Research Fellow Program](https://kingcenter.stanford.edu/opportunities/undergraduate-students/academic-year-part-time-undergraduate-research-fellow-program)</span>
- <span class="program-name">[DataSURF (Data Science Undergraduate Research Focus)](https://datasciencemajor.stanford.edu/research-impact/data-science-undergraduate-research-focus)</span>
- <span class="program-name">[SESUR (Sustainability, Engineering and Science Undergraduate Research)](https://sesur.stanford.edu/)</span>

If you are not a Stanford undergraduate but currently a student at a different University or Community College, consider Stanford's [SURGE program](https://sustainability.stanford.edu/our-community/access-belonging-community/surge){: .cardinal-link}.

I occasionally hire undergraduate RAs for specific projects and will post any openings here.
